About these results

  • We don’t ask for names. This page shows totals only, never one person’s answers.
  • Groups smaller than 5 people are hidden, because on roads this size they could point to a household.
  • Written answers are grouped into themes by hand, so the themes and quotes catch up a little after new answers arrive.
  • We’ll write a full report for the Kincora Residents Committee once the survey closes.
